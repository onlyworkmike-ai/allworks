About Me

I am a graphic and motion designer focused on visually appealing content, innovation and attention to detail.

I design so a brand's message lands clearly, aim to exceed what clients expect, and keep up with design trends and new technology.

Before design I studied architecture. My work was featured in the book and exhibition "1 Are House" by Indonesian Young Architects at UMA Seminyak, Bali.
